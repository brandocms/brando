- A video's playback settings in a gallery block (Autoplay, Loop, Muted, Controls, Lazy load) now apply on the public page and in the live preview; they show Default / On / Off, as the video drawer does, and Default follows the video.
  Gallery video settings saved before this release never reached the page, and they now show as Default. Set again any you want to override.

- An open live preview shows a video's changed Autoplay, Controls or Lazy load setting at once, and its Loop or Muted setting too unless the site's player has replaced the `<video>`; before, it kept the running player until the preview was reopened.
