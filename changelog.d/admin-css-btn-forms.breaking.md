- The legacy `a.primary`, `a.secondary`, `.tertiary` and `.danger` button styles are removed
  ([#3208](https://github.com/brandocms/brando/pull/3208)).
  Change `primary` to `btn btn--primary`, `secondary` to `btn`, `tertiary` to `btn btn--quiet
  btn--sm`, `danger` to `btn btn--danger`, `primary danger` to `btn btn--primary btn--danger` and
  `small` to `btn--sm`; `fullWidth`, `narrow` and `fw` have no replacement.
