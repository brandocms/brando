- A synchronized translation saves after its source both removed a block and added another: the form no longer keeps the removed block, which made the save fail as a change to content the source controls. A removed block, or a removed child block, now also leaves the translation when nothing was added.
- A synchronized translation open in the form keeps the source's changes it loaded, and the editor's unsaved work, when the source is saved again and the form is drawn again (another editor opening the entry): it takes the newer changes after its save, as the guide says, instead of loading them over the work and then refusing the save.
- An editor who opens an entry while another editor's unsaved change has moved a child block into another container, a synchronized translation's loaded source changes included, sees the child once, where it was moved to: the form showed it in both containers, and the page failed.
