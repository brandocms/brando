- A video's Preload setting is called Lazy load, and the Edit video form shows it and the other playback settings as Default / On / Off, as the video drawer does, so saving a video no longer stores Off for every setting left alone.

- Without JavaScript, a video with controls or autoplay has them in its `<noscript>` fallback too. A video that is not lazy no longer has a `<noscript>` copy beside it: its own `<video>` has the source.
