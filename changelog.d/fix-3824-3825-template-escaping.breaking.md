- A `has_many` or `has_one` row's field that the owner's subform draws as plain text prints as text in templates that reach the row through its owner (with `{{ }}` or `{% editable_field %}`), also when the row schema's own form draws it as rich text, so what an editor typed in the owner's textarea no longer prints as live markup (a link, or an event handler breaking out of an attribute). The same record printed as an entry of its own, or reached through a subform a component draws (`inputs_for :items do component MyAppAdmin.Items end`, which draws no field itself), still prints by its own forms. When one record is reached through two owners' rules and the lists are combined (`concat`), a field prints as rich text only if both rules say so.
  To keep printing such a field's markup, draw it as `:rich_text` in the owner's subform: `inputs_for :items do input :text, :rich_text end`.
- A plain field passed straight to a tag option that prints markup is printed as text: `caption` on `{% picture %}`, and `caption`, `title` and `play_button` on `{% video %}` (`{ caption: entry.title }` printed a tag in the title as markup). A rich-text field still prints as sanitized markup, and a literal written in the template as it is.
  To keep printing such a field's markup, make the field rich text (draw it as `:rich_text`), or opt in for the template with `{% assign c = entry.title | raw %}` and pass `c`.
