- **A page's subpages open as a tree, and its fragments as chips.** Child
  rows hang from their parent with a connector, in the parent's columns, so
  their status and author sit under the page's; the chevron that opens them
  is in front of the title, with "3 pages · 2 fragments" beside it.
  Fragments, which belong to the page rather than to the page tree, are one
  line of chips after the subpages, each with its key and menu, ending in
  "Create fragment". Other listings with children, such as block modules,
  get the same tree.

- **`child_listing` takes `style: :chips` and `add:`**, to show a listing's
  children as one line of small cards with an add button. See the Child
  listings section of the Blueprint listings guide.
