- **A denser, steadier content listing.** Rows are about 60px instead of 79px
  on desktop, with a 14px title. An entry's status is a badge in its tone
  (published green, pending amber, deactivated red, draft grey), and the
  entry header's status menu takes the same tint. The button that opens a
  row's children has a column of its own before the status, so it lines up
  from row to row. The row's actions menu shows ⋯ instead of a chevron (also
  in revisions and the export menu). The footer sits under the last row
  instead of at the bottom of the window. A listing whose blueprint adds
  filters lays its toolbar out in two rows. On a phone a row is two lines.

- **On a phone the presence avatars are hidden**, on every screen: fixed in
  the bottom right corner, they covered rows, footers and form fields.

- **A page heading's actions sit on the title's line**, also when a subtitle
  follows it; on a phone they go under the subtitle.
