- A project made with `mix brando.install` shows its video blocks: its frontend starts Jupiter's `SmartVideo`, which loads a lazy video as it nears the viewport, reveals the wrapper the installed CSS hides, plays an autoplay video while it is in view, and plays a Bunny or Mux HLS stream through `hls.js` in a browser without native HLS. In the installed CSS, a video's cover no longer blocks its play button, and the cover and the button hide while the video plays.
