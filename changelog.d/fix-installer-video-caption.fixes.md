- In a project made with `mix brando.install`, a video's cover and play button sit on the video, so the cover no longer hides its caption and the button is centred on the video; a video with controls shows no cover over them. To fix an existing project, copy the `[data-smart-video]` rules from Brando's `priv/templates/brando.install/assets/frontend/css/app.css` into `assets/frontend/css/app.css`.
