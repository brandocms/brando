- The `raw` filter no longer turns text an editor typed into rich text (a rich-text field, a subform row's, a text ref's text, a picture's or image's title and caption, a `rich_text` var or global) into live markup: it prints rich text as it prints without `raw`, also after a filter, for each piece of a `split`, and through `assign`.
  A subform row's rich text prints as markup without `raw`: remove `| raw` after it (`{{ row.text | raw }}` becomes `{{ row.text }}`). Rich text a template `capture`s with other text around it (`<div>{{ entry.intro }}</div>`) is decoded by `raw` as before, so do not print such a capture with `raw`.
