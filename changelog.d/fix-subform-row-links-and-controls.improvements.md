- An `embeds_many` or `has_many, cast: true` subform draws its drag handle only when the relation has `sort_param:` and its remove button only when it has `drop_param:`, since without them a reorder or a removal was cast away on save; such a relation now gets a compile warning. Transformer subforms and custom `component` subforms keep their controls.
  Add the options the warning names to each relation it lists, for example `relation :links, :embeds_many, module: MyApp.Link, sort_param: :sort_links_ids, drop_param: :drop_links_ids` (a `has_many` also needs `cast: true`).
