- In a Liquid template, a list that is nil (a gallery with no images, a field left empty) reads as an empty list, as in Liquid: a `for` loop over it runs zero times and renders its `else` branch, `sort`, `sort_natural`, `where`, `map`, `compact`, `reverse`, `uniq` and `partition` give an empty list, `concat` adds nothing for it, `join` gives an empty string, and `first` and `last` give nothing. In 0.54 these raised an error while the page rendered; the block now renders, with the list empty.
