- Undelete on a trashed draft whose address another entry took meanwhile brings the draft back with a suffix on its address (`about-1`), as for a published entry, instead of crashing the listing; an address that still can't be given, or an entry no longer in the trash, shows a message and leaves the entry as it is.

- A restored entry is given a free address in the scope its type declares: a page whose address only a page in another language holds keeps it, and one whose address is held in two languages no longer fails.
