- An open form takes in what another tab saved, so a save or recovery copy from it no longer removes the other tab's has_many picks or blanks `publish_at`. When both tabs changed the same list, including a list a tab recovers on reconnect after the other tab saved, the form keeps its own and asks which to keep.
