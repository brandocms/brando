- EuropaCSS `0.1.0-beta.2` (`@brandocms/europacss-cli` and `@brandocms/vite-plugin-europacss`) in the generated frontend and backend: token maps take breakpoint collection keys, and `scale-from(n, breakpoint)` keeps a value in px below a breakpoint and scales it from there.
  `mix brando.doctor` warns about a backend on `0.1.0-beta.0`; `mix brando.gen.backend --upgrade` and `mix brando.assets.setup --backend-only` move it.
  Two layout changes reach the application's admin CSS: `@grid` follows the admin preset's `columns.count` (6 columns below 768px, 16 above) instead of 12, and a fractional span such as `@column 2.5/16` comes out wider. Compile `assets/backend/css/app.css` with `pnpm exec europa css/app.css --config europa.json5 -o before.css` before the upgrade and again after, and diff; write `0.5:-0.5/16` for a span that keeps its old width.
