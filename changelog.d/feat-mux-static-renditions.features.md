- A Mux video's **Quality** can play an MP4 static rendition (270p up to 2160p) instead of the stream, as Bunny videos can: new Mux uploads ask for 720p and 1080p renditions (`config :brando, Brando.Videos, mux_static_renditions: [720, 1080]`), and a video offers the ones Mux reports ready. A video config's legacy `mp4_support: "standard"` now asks for these renditions rather than `highest`.
  Existing Mux videos stream until they have renditions. Run `mix brando.videos.mux_renditions` to list the videos that would play an MP4 (an autoplaying video without controls or a play button under Automatic quality, or a fixed MP4 quality on the video or a placement) and lack renditions, with what it would do: on an older asset it first deletes the `highest` rendition or sets legacy `mp4_support` to `none`, which Mux requires. Videos that only stream are skipped. Then run `mix brando.videos.mux_renditions --apply`. In a release, run `bin/my_app rpc "Brando.Videos.Uploaders.Mux.add_static_renditions(dry_run: true) |> IO.inspect()"` first, then without `dry_run: true`; each video's result is logged.
